# Proposal

## Why

The player is a 16:9 video frame and a row of text buttons, so a video picture takes the screen and the upcoming songs are not one gesture away. Someone listening on a phone needs the current song, the queue, and sound without a video, while playlist and playback rules stay as they are.

## What Changes

- Replace the current player chrome (the visible 16:9 frame and the compact bar on other routes) with a mini player, a now-playing view, and a queue sheet. Opening and closing these surfaces does not stop playback.
- **BREAKING** The compact bar no longer offers the full control set at a smaller size. While a queue is active, a mini player is fixed to the bottom of every route, including `/entertainment/music`. It shows a small square still, the song title, and the playlist name on one line (ellipsis, no wrap), play/pause, and a thin progress bar along the top edge. That bar is not a second seek control. Tapping the bar, outside play/pause, opens now playing. Play/pause reflects the current state and does not open the view. When nothing is queued, the bar is absent and the shell does not reserve space for it. Reloading still restores the library and does not autoplay.
- Add a now-playing view opened from the mini player. On the Music page it is the player region: the library and track list stay behind it, and closing returns to them. A close control returns to the mini player and does not pause. The view shows a large square still generated in the app from the song title (a colored tile with the title's initials). It does not load the YouTube thumbnail and does not play video in that space. Under the cover: song title, playlist name, elapsed time, a seek bar, and remaining time. Dragging the bar seeks. Arrow keys move it.
- One transport row, in this order: shuffle, previous, play/pause, next, repeat. Play/pause is the primary control and is larger than the others. Shuffle and repeat show their current mode (off, all, and one for repeat). Previous on the first song restarts it. Next on the last song stops unless repeat is on. Shuffle does not repeat a song until the cycle ends. Volume and mute sit on the transport row above `48rem`. At `48rem` and below they move to a second row so the transport row does not scroll sideways. Targets are at least 44px.
- Put the queue on the now-playing surface, not in an overflow menu and not on its own route. A drag handle and an **Up next** label sit at the bottom. A swipe up, or the Queue control (click or keyboard), opens the queue. A swipe down or the close control returns to now playing. Playback continues through both. The sheet has two groups: **Now playing** (the current song) and **Next** (the rest, in play order). The current row is marked. Choosing a later row starts playback there and continues through the rest of the queue. Shuffle, repeat, and a song change update **Next**. An empty playlist has no queue and cannot be opened. If the official player reports a video is unavailable, that row shows the error and the queue moves on.
- Play audio only. The mini player, now playing, the queue, and the bar on other routes show no video picture and no YouTube player chrome. The page never navigates to YouTube and never shows the watch page, a title bar, related videos, or an end screen. Sound still comes from the official YouTube IFrame Player API. The iframe is the audio engine only: no 16:9 frame, no letterbox, and no visible player chrome. This is an audio presentation, not an ad overlay. Do not add a skip-ad control, cover an advertisement, or switch to youtube-dl, yt-dlp, Invidious, Piped, or a direct media URL. If the iframe API fails to load, the transport stays visible and a short message says playback is unavailable. The Music page, the player, and the queue still contain no advertisement, sponsor slot, banner, or skip-ad control.
- Use the shell's existing `48rem` breakpoint. At `48rem` and below, the playlist library and the track list stack in one column and nothing in the player scrolls sideways. The mini player is full width above the safe area, and page content clears it so the last track is not hidden. Now playing is a full-screen sheet. The cover scales down so the title, seek bar, transport, and **Up next** handle fit without page scroll on a 320px-wide screen. The queue is a sheet over now playing. Dialogs for new playlist, edit, delete, and add song stay inside the viewport and can be scrolled if the keyboard is open. Above `48rem`, the Music page keeps three regions (library, tracks, and the player). The player region uses the now-playing layout. The queue can sit under the cover or open as the same sheet, and it stays one action away. Other routes show only the mini player, not a second video row. Light mode, dark mode, and the collapsed sidebar keep their current behavior. The player uses the same tokens as the rest of the shell. Copy the now-playing structure (large artwork, title, source name, seek, one transport row, queue one gesture away, mini bar when the full view is closed). Do not copy Spotify's name, logo, wordmark, green, type, or artwork.
- Out of scope: Spotify login, Spotify's catalog, Connect, lyrics, Canvas, and the Spotify visual design; dragging queue rows into a new order; video playback, fullscreen video, and picture-in-picture; downloading audio or bypassing YouTube's player; changes to how playlists and songs are saved.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `mini-player`: The bar fixed to the bottom of every route while a queue is active. Still artwork, one-line title and playlist name, play/pause, and a thin progress indicator. Tapping the bar opens now playing. The bar is absent when nothing is queued, shows no video picture, and stays full width above the safe area at `48rem` and below.
- `now-playing`: The now-playing surface and the Up next queue sheet. Still cover, seek, the transport row, volume by breakpoint, queue groups, and close without pausing. Audio only: no video picture and no YouTube chrome, with the official iframe kept out of the layout. On the Music page this surface is the player region. At `48rem` and below it is a full-screen sheet that fits on a 320px-wide screen, and playlist dialogs stay usable with the keyboard.

### Modified Capabilities

None. `openspec list --specs` is empty. Playlist create, edit, and delete, accepted YouTube links, `localStorage`, playback order, repeat, and shuffle live on the completed `music-player` change (`music-library`, `music-player`), not under `openspec/specs/`. This change does not alter those rules or `entertainment-menu`. It replaces the visible player and the compact bar that `music-player` describes.

## Impact

- Replaces the chrome in `src/music/PlayerBar.tsx` and the way `src/shell/AppShell.tsx` mounts it. The player region on `src/pages/MusicPage.tsx` uses the now-playing layout instead of a video frame.
- `src/music/youtube-player.ts` stays the playback engine. The iframe is not shown as a 16:9 frame. No new playback dependency, and no youtube-dl, yt-dlp, Invidious, Piped, or direct media URL.
- The queue sheet reads the existing order from `src/music/queue.ts`. It does not change how playlists and songs are saved in `src/music/storage.ts`.
- While a queue is active, page content clears the mini player, including on Home, Health, Projects, Settings, and Account. Those pages are otherwise unchanged. The Entertainment menu is unchanged.
- Layout uses the existing `48rem` breakpoint and the current light and dark tokens. No new backend, account API, or deploy pipeline.
